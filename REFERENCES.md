## Data and published values

- Filter response curves for the matched bands (`tools/svo/*.dat`, converted into `web/src/engine/filters.json` by `tools/build-filters.mjs`): SVO Filter Profile Service, https://svo2.cab.inta-csic.es/theory/fps/ (Rodrigo, Solano & Bayo 2012; Rodrigo & Solano 2020), filters 2MASS/2MASS.J, 2MASS/2MASS.H, 2MASS/2MASS.Ks (Cohen, Wheaton & Megeath 2003, AJ 126, 1090) and WISE/WISE.W1, WISE/WISE.W2 (Wright et al. 2010, AJ 140, 1868; Jarrett et al. 2011, ApJ 735, 112).
- SPHEREx Level 2 images and the pointing index built from their headers: NASA/IPAC Infrared Science Archive, public S3 bucket `nasa-irsa-spherex` (releases qr2, qr3, dr1).
